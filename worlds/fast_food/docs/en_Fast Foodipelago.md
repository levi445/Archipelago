# Fast Foodipelago

## What in the world is this?

Do you love being broke and having diabetes? If so, Fast Foodipelago is the randomizer for you.
It adds random fast food items to the item pool as checks. The player has to physically go to the fast food location, 
order the item(s) and eat the item(s). Then, they may check the item off the list.
So, if you were dying to eat a Whopper, now you have a great excuse: you have to do this so your friend doesn't get BK'ed.
The goal is to either reach a certain number of calories eaten, eat a certain number of items, or both.
Restaurant unlocks and calorie boosts are added into the multiworld pool for other players to find.
Currently uses the honor system — mark items as eaten after eating them IRL.

### Currently Supported restaurants
McDonald's, Burger King, Wendy's, Taco Bell, Pizza Hut, Papa John's, Domino's, Sonic, Arby's, Hardee's,
Dairy Queen, KFC, Chick-fil-A, Popeyes, Subway, Culver's
