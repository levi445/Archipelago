# Fast Foodipelago

## What in the world is this?

Do you love being broke and having diabetes? If so, Fast Foodipelago is the randomizer for you.
It adds random fast food items to the item pool as checks. The player has to physically go to the fast food location, 
order the item(s) and eat the item(s). Then, they may check the item off the list.
So, if you were dying to eat a Whopper, now you have a great excuse: you have to do this so your friend doesn't get BK'ed.
The goal is to either reach a certain number of calories eaten or eat all items or both.
Restaurant unlocks and additional calories are added into the pool for other players to find.
Currently, uses the honor system. You mark things as eaten after eating them IRL.
